What CatchLane does

CatchLane captures quick notes from your phone (share sheet and voice stubs in the MVP) and sorts them into Ideas, Errands, and Follow-ups. The Android MVP uses on-device demo data and local heuristics — no wearable required.

Data we process

  • Captures you enter: Text/voice notes you save stay on-device in this MVP (Capacitor Preferences / local storage).
  • Demo content: Sample captures ship in the app for UI testing and are not your personal history.
  • Preferences: Free weekly capture counters and an optional demo Plus flag are stored on-device.
  • Purchases: Subscriptions/lifetime unlocks use RevenueCat and Google Play Billing. RevenueCat processes purchase tokens and anonymous app user ids per their policy. We do not use AdMob.

What we do not do

  • No sale of your notes
  • No continuous microphone listening in this MVP
  • No cloud sync of captures in this MVP
