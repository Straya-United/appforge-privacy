Overview

AusCite is an Australian civic Q&A app that shows informational answers with links to public official sources. We designed the MVP to keep personal data minimal.

Honesty about answers: In this MVP, questions stay on your device and are matched to local / mock answer content. AusCite does not run live AI legal research against the internet for each question. Answers are informational only and are not legal, tax, insurance, or medical advice.

Data we process

On your device

  • Daily ask counter — stored in local storage to enforce the free daily limit (3 asks/day).
  • Purchase stub / Pro flag — local marker for the RevenueCat entitlement pro during development. Production builds use RevenueCat + Google Play Billing.

Not collected by the MVP web/app shell

  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not use AdMob or third-party advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell personal information.

When you go Pro (production)

Google Play Billing and RevenueCat process purchase receipts and anonymous/app-user identifiers needed to unlock the pro entitlement. Their policies apply to payment processing.

Questions you type

In this MVP, questions stay on-device and are matched to local mock answers. A future cloud answer service would process question text to generate replies; if that ships, this policy will be updated before enablement.

Permissions

The Android shell may use standard network access (INTERNET) to load fonts and open official source URLs in a browser, and for Play Billing / RevenueCat when purchases are enabled. No SMS, contacts, precise location, or microphone access is required for the MVP.

Children

AusCite is aimed at adults dealing with tenancy, insurance, Medicare, and tax topics. It is not directed at children under 13.
