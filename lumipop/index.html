Privacy Policy — LUMIPOP

Last updated: 23 September 2026
App: com.appforgedigital.lumipop (Android and iOS)
Contact: vnss666@gmail.com

Overview. LUMIPOP is a match-3 puzzle game. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and your game progress never leaves your device. The app is free to play and supported by ads, with an optional one-time purchase that removes them.

What the game stores

Your scores, campaign and daily-mission progress, saved in-progress games, and your sound and effects settings. All of it is stored locally on your device in the app's own storage. LUMIPOP does not upload it, and we cannot see it.

Advertising

LUMIPOP shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google receives information from your device, which may include your device's advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID, or on iPhone and iPad the IDFA if you allow tracking), IP address, device and operating system details, coarse location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with an ad. This information is handled by Google, not by us; see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services.

On iPhone and iPad, LUMIPOP asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the ad SDK starts. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the advertising identifier is not shared and you still get ads, just not personalised ones. You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised advertising, in your device settings under Settings › Privacy › Ads.

Purchases

Removing ads is a one-time purchase made through Google Play Billing on Android or the App Store on iPhone and iPad. The purchase is processed by Google or Apple, and its status is validated through our billing provider, RevenueCat, so the app knows the purchase is active. This involves purchase and receipt records and an anonymous identifier RevenueCat assigns to the installation.

We never see or receive your payment details. The app does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal detail.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load ads, check purchase status, and load the game's web font.
  • Advertising ID (Android) / App Tracking Transparency (iOS) — used by the AdMob SDK to deliver and measure ads, as described above. On iOS this is only used if you allow it.
  • Billing — to make the Remove Ads purchase through Google Play or the App Store.

The game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions.

Playing offline

The game itself works without a network connection. Ads and purchases need one; if the device is offline, LUMIPOP simply carries on without them, and a Remove Ads purchase you have already made stays active.

Children

LUMIPOP is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes everything the game has stored on your device, including your progress. A Remove Ads purchase is tied to your Google account (Android) or Apple ID (iOS) and can be restored from the app's home screen after reinstalling.

Changes

If this policy changes, the Last updated date above changes with it.
