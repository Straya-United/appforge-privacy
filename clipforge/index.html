Overview

ClipForge helps creators forge short App Store previews and Reels from URLs or screenshots. This policy describes how the current MVP handles data. ClipForge does not require an account.

What we collect

ClipForge does not upload your screenshots or project drafts to ClipForge servers in this MVP. Data you enter or select (URLs, on-device screenshot picks, timeline layout, export counters) is stored locally on your device.

Export / HD / credits counters are local MVP stubs — on-device UI and local draft/export flow, not cloud processing of your media.

Purchases

If you buy ClipForge Pro or credit packs, Google Play Billing and RevenueCat process the transaction. We receive entitlement / purchase status needed to unlock features — not your full payment card details.

Permissions

  • Photos / files — only when you choose screenshots to import.
  • Internet — to load a product URL preview stub and to validate purchases on device builds.

We do not use these permissions for advertising. No AdMob.

Third parties

  • RevenueCat + Google Play for purchases.
  • No AdMob or ad networks in this MVP.
  • No analytics SDK in the MVP scaffold. If that changes, this policy will be updated before release.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13.

Data deletion & your choices

Uninstalling the App removes locally stored ClipForge data from that device. Clearing app storage also deletes drafts and counters. Manage Play subscriptions separately in Google Play.
