Last updated: 10 September 2026

What RingWhy does

RingWhy helps you guess why a missed or unknown call might have rung and draft a short SMS reply. The Android MVP uses simulated demo call data and an on-device heuristics stub. Real device call-log permission is deferred.

Data we process

  • On-device heuristics: Classification runs locally from demo call metadata (number display, optional notes, time-of-day). We do not record or upload call audio.
  • Stub call log: Demo numbers are fake AU-style samples shipped in the app for UI testing. They are not your real call history.
  • Preferences: Weekly free-lookup counters and an optional demo Plus flag are stored on-device via Capacitor Preferences.
  • Purchases: Subscriptions/lifetime unlocks use RevenueCat and Google Play Billing. RevenueCat processes purchase tokens and anonymous app user ids per their policy. We do not use AdMob.

What we do not do

  • No sale of personal call data
  • No call audio capture
  • No claim of carrier-level caller ID databases in this MVP
