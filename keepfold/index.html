KeepFold Privacy Policy

AppForge Digital (Sydney) · Last updated: 10 September 2026

KeepFold (“the App”) is a household-safe photo declutter tool. This policy explains what we collect, how media is handled, and how billing works.

Summary

  • Photos and videos are processed on your device for declutter features.
  • This MVP uses local stubs / heuristics for duplicate clustering and protect tags — media is not uploaded to AppForge servers for analysis.
  • We do not use AdMob or third-party advertising SDKs.
  • Subscriptions and lifetime unlocks are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat.

Data we process

Photos & media (on-device)

When gallery access is granted on a native build, the App may read media metadata and content locally to group near-duplicates and apply protect tags (receipt, ID, kids, warranty). In the current MVP, web preview uses demo media stubs; clustering is heuristic and protect tags use lightweight OCR-style heuristics — not a claim of full on-device computer vision.

We do not sell your photos. We do not use your gallery for advertising.

App preferences

Settings such as onboarding completion, scan mode, demo Plus unlock, and keep/trash queues may be stored locally (e.g. Capacitor Preferences) on your device.

Purchases

If you buy KeepFold Plus, Google Play processes payment. RevenueCat manages entitlements (entitlement id plus). RevenueCat may receive purchase tokens / anonymous app user identifiers as needed to unlock Plus. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Diagnostics

Google Play may collect standard install and crash diagnostics according to your device and Play settings. The App does not include a separate advertising analytics SDK.

Permissions

  • Photos / media — required for declutter on native builds (stubbed in web preview).
  • Internet — used for Google Play Billing and RevenueCat entitlement checks when configured.

Children

KeepFold is a general-audience utility. It is not directed at children under 13. The “kids” protect tag exists to help households avoid deleting family photos of minors — it is not a children’s social product.

Data retention & deletion

Local preferences remain on device until you clear app data or uninstall. Purchase records are retained by Google Play / RevenueCat per their policies. Uninstalling the App removes local app storage on your device.

Your choices

  • Deny Photos permission — declutter features that need the gallery will not run (demo stubs may still preview UI).
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play subscriptions.
  • Contact us to ask privacy questions (below).

International

AppForge Digital is based in Sydney, Australia. Billing providers may process data in other regions as described in their policies.

Changes

We may update this policy as the App gains real gallery pipelines or OCR. Material changes will be reflected in this document and the Play listing when required.
