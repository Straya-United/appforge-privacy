Privacy Policy — GridFire

Last updated: 11 September 2026
App: com.appforgedigital.gridfire
Contact: vnss666@gmail.com

Overview. GridFire is a puzzle game with no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress stays on your device. The game is free with ads, and a single optional purchase removes them.

What the game stores on your device

Your scores, level progress, achievements, unlocked skins and backdrops, and your chosen nickname are saved in the app's own storage on your device. We cannot see any of it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Online play and leaderboards

GridFire includes online race modes and leaderboards, but they are not enabled in this release — the app reports them as coming soon and sends nothing to any server. If a future update switches them on, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what is sent, which would be your chosen nickname, a random device identifier and your score or in-match progress. No real-name or account information would ever be involved.

Advertising

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure them, Google receives information from your device, which may include your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system details, and how you interact with an ad. This is handled by Google, not by us; see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use their services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised advertising, in Settings › Privacy › Ads. Rewarded ads are always optional — watching one only ever grants an extra life.

Purchases

Removing ads is a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment, and our billing provider RevenueCat verifies it so the purchase can be restored later. That involves a purchase receipt and an anonymous identifier for the installation. We never see your payment details, and the app never asks for your name or email address.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load ads and check purchase status.
  • Advertising ID — used by the AdMob SDK as described above.
  • Billing — to make the Remove Ads purchase through Google Play.
  • Vibration — tap feedback.

No location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions are requested.

Children

GridFire is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes everything stored on your device, including your progress. A Remove Ads purchase is tied to your Google account and can be restored after reinstalling.

Changes

If this policy changes, the Last updated date above changes with it.
