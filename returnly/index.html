Privacy Policy — Returnly

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Returnly ("we", "the app") is a local-first mobile application that helps you track return, warranty, and expiry deadlines.

Summary

  • Receipt and product photos you capture are stored locally on your device.
  • Item metadata (name, merchant, dates, notes) is stored locally in on-device Preferences storage.
  • This MVP does not upload images or item data to Returnly servers.
  • OCR is an on-device stub in this MVP: images are not sent to a cloud OCR service. Draft fields are suggestions only — you must review them before saving.
  • Optional local notifications are scheduled on your device only.
  • Plus / shared vault does not sync to a cloud backend yet (stub).
  • No AdMob and no advertising SDKs.

Data we process on-device

  • Photos you choose from camera or gallery (optional per item) — kept on device for receipts / labels
  • Item fields you enter or confirm (name, merchant, deadline type, dates, notes) — Preferences
  • App settings (plan, reminder preferences) — Preferences

Permissions

  • Camera / Photos — to capture or pick receipt and product images (stored locally)
  • Notifications — to remind you before deadlines (optional; you can deny)
  • Exact alarms / boot completed / vibrate — to schedule and restore local reminders
  • Internet — only for RevenueCat / Google Play Billing when purchases are configured

Purchases (third parties)

When configured, purchases go through RevenueCat and Google Play Billing. Purchase history is handled by those platforms under their policies. Returnly does not sell your data.

Advertising

Returnly does not use AdMob or other ad networks.

Children's privacy

Returnly is not directed at children under 13.

Data retention and deletion

Delete items in-app to remove their local records. Uninstalling the app removes local app storage.

Future cloud features

If a future version adds cloud sync or real cloud OCR APIs, this policy will be updated before those features process personal data off-device, and you will be asked for consent where required.
