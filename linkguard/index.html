What LinkGuard does

LinkGuard lets you share any URL for a plain-English phishing/risk score before you tap. The Android MVP uses on-device heuristic stubs and demo URLs — it is not a full threat-intel feed.

Data we process

  • URLs you submit: Links you paste or share for a score are processed on-device in this MVP. History of recent checks may be stored locally.
  • Demo links: Sample URLs ship in the app for UI testing.
  • Preferences: Free weekly check counters and an optional demo Plus flag are stored on-device via Capacitor Preferences.
  • Purchases: Subscriptions/lifetime unlocks use RevenueCat and Google Play Billing. RevenueCat processes purchase tokens and anonymous app user ids per their policy. We do not use AdMob.

What we do not do

  • No sale of URLs you check
  • No claim of carrier-grade threat intelligence in this MVP
  • No automatic scanning of your entire browsing history
