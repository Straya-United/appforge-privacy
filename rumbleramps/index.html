Privacy Policy — Rumble Ramps

Last updated: 25 September 2026
App: com.appforgedigital.rumbleramps (Android and iOS)
Contact: admin@appforgedigital.com.au

For parents and carers. Rumble Ramps is a one-thumb toy driving game made for children aged about 4 to 8. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way to share anything with other people. Medals, rides, toys and settings are stored only on the device. We do not collect your child's name, email, photos, voice, location or any other personal information, and we cannot see anything they do in the game.

The game is free to play with a small number of child-safe ads, and one optional purchase — made by a grown-up — unlocks three more worlds and turns the ads off.

What the game stores on the device

Adventure medals and best times, unlocked rides, paint colours, stickers and helmets, the toy scrapbook and parcel-run stamps, and the sound, music, effects and battery-saver settings. All of it lives in the app's own storage on the device. It is never uploaded, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Advertising

Rumble Ramps shows ads supplied by Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen only, and occasionally a full-screen ad between adventures — never during a ride. Every ad request is marked as coming from a child-directed app, is limited to content rated suitable for general audiences, and is non-personalised: no interest-based or behavioural advertising and no remarketing.

The Android version does not request the Advertising ID permission, and the iOS version never asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency), so no advertising identifier is shared. To serve an ad, Google still receives limited technical information such as the IP address, device model and operating system, which it uses only to deliver the ad, count it and prevent fraud. Google's handling of this is described in Google's Privacy Policy and its child-directed treatment documentation.

The optional purchase

"The full toy box" is a one-time purchase that opens Garden Giggles, Cookie Kitchen and Moonlight Playpark and removes the ads. Before any purchase screen appears, the game asks a grown-up question that a young child is unlikely to answer. The purchase itself is made through Google Play Billing on Android or the Apple App Store on iOS, using the device owner's account; we never see or receive payment details.

So the game knows the purchase is active, its status is checked through our billing provider, RevenueCat. This involves the store's purchase/receipt record and an anonymous identifier RevenueCat assigns to the installation — no name, email or other personal detail. A purchase can be restored from Settings after reinstalling.

Permissions

  • Internet — to load ads and check the purchase status. The whole game works offline; ads and purchases simply wait for a connection.
  • Billing — for the optional purchase through Google Play or the App Store.

The game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, storage or advertising-ID permissions.

No links out, no social features

There are no links to websites, social networks or other apps inside the game, no chat, no user profiles and no way to send or receive messages or pictures.

Children's privacy

Rumble Ramps is directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game is designed so that it has no way to. If you believe any personal information about your child has reached us, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the Last updated date above changes with it.
