What MailTriage does

MailTriage turns an overnight inbox into a short digest: 5 actions, 3 FYIs, and 1 can-wait. The Android MVP uses simulated demo emails and an on-device triage stub — real mailbox OAuth is deferred.

Data we process

  • Demo inbox: Fake sample messages ship in the app for UI testing. They are not your real email.
  • On-device triage: Classification runs locally on demo metadata in this MVP. We do not upload mailbox contents.
  • Preferences: Free weekly digest counters and an optional demo Plus flag are stored on-device via Capacitor Preferences.
  • Purchases: Subscriptions/lifetime unlocks use RevenueCat and Google Play Billing. RevenueCat processes purchase tokens and anonymous app user ids per their policy. We do not use AdMob.

What we do not do

  • No sale of email content
  • No live Gmail/Outlook access in this MVP
  • No reading of your device mail accounts without a future explicit OAuth flow
