SoloBrief helps freelancers capture voice notes and turn them into summaries and action items. Package: com.solobrief.app.

Summary

  • Built for individual freelancers.
  • Microphone audio is captured only when you record and grant permission.
  • Transcripts in this MVP come from a local stub / mock transcription path (not live third-party cloud STT / Whisper).
  • Briefs stay on device in local storage.
  • Plus purchases go through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat (entitlement plus).
  • We do not sell personal data.

Microphone audio

Collected when you tap record with RECORD_AUDIO granted. Used only to create your brief. This MVP processes audio through an on-device stub transcription pipeline and does not upload recordings to SoloBrief servers. Audio is not used for ads.

Local transcripts and notes

Transcript text, bullets, action items, timers, and free-minute counters stay in local app storage until you clear data or uninstall.

Purchases (RevenueCat / Google Play)

If you buy SoloBrief Plus (subscription or lifetime), Google Play processes payment. RevenueCat receives purchase/entitlement signals so the app can unlock unlimited minutes. We do not receive your full card number.

Notifications and network

POST_NOTIFICATIONS may be used for optional local brief-ready alerts. INTERNET is used for Play Billing / RevenueCat and future features.

Permissions

  • RECORD_AUDIO — capture voice notes
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS — reliable recording routing
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — optional local alerts
  • INTERNET — billing / RevenueCat

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Limited sharing with Google Play, RevenueCat, parties you choose via Share sheet, or when required by law.

Retention and children

Local briefs remain until deleted, storage cleared, or uninstall. Manage Play subscriptions in Google Play. Not directed at children under 13.
