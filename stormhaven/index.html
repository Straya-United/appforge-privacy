Privacy Policy — Stormhaven

Last updated: 13 September 2026
App: com.appforgedigital.stormhaven
Contact: vnss666@gmail.com

Overview. Stormhaven is a coastal rescue strategy game. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and your campaign progress never leaves your device unless you choose to join the optional weekly community goal. The game is free to play and supported by ads, with an optional one-time purchase that removes them.

What the game stores

Your campaign stars and island unlocks, the islands you have renamed, harbour restoration progress, daily rescue results and earned boat colours, and your sound and reduced-motion settings. All of it is stored locally on your device in the app's own storage. Stormhaven does not upload it, and we cannot see it.

You can export a backup of your progress to a file you choose, and restore it later. That file stays wherever you save it; the game does not send it anywhere.

The optional weekly community goal

Stormhaven has a shared weekly rescue total that players can choose to contribute to. It is entirely opt-in: until you join, the game never contacts the service, and everything else works offline.

If you join, the game creates a random token on your device and sends that token, the current week, and your best rescue counts per mission to our service, which is hosted on Supabase. The token is hashed before it is stored, and it is not linked to your name, your Google account, or any other identifier. The service only ever reports back the week, the shared total and the goal — there is no ranking, no prize and no public list of players.

Leaving removes that device's contributions from the shared total. If you are offline when you leave, the game stops sharing immediately and completes the removal when it next reconnects. Progress backups do not include the community token.

Advertising

Stormhaven shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google receives information from your device, which may include your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system details, coarse location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with an ad. This information is handled by Google, not by us; see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised advertising, in your device settings under Settings › Privacy › Ads.

Purchases

Removing ads is a one-time purchase made through Google Play Billing. The purchase is processed by Google, and its status is validated through our billing provider, RevenueCat, so the app knows the purchase is active. This involves purchase and receipt records and an anonymous identifier RevenueCat assigns to the installation.

We never see or receive your payment details. The app does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal detail.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load ads, check purchase status, and contribute to the weekly community goal if you have joined.
  • Advertising ID — used by the AdMob SDK to deliver and measure ads, as described above.
  • Billing — to make the Remove Ads purchase through Google Play.

The game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions. Saving and restoring a progress backup uses the Android file picker, which gives the app access only to the single file you pick.

Playing offline

The whole campaign and the daily rescue work without a network connection. Ads, purchases and the community goal need one; if the device is offline, Stormhaven simply carries on without them, and a Remove Ads purchase you have already made stays active.

Children

Stormhaven is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes everything the game has stored on your device, including your progress and the community token. A Remove Ads purchase is tied to your Google account and can be restored from the app's settings after reinstalling.

Changes

If this policy changes, the Last updated date above changes with it.
