Overview

Sub Radar helps you spot recurring charges and rank subscriptions to cancel. We design for local-first use on your device.

Data we process

  • On device: subscription lists, imported statement stub metadata, and app settings stored via Capacitor Preferences on your device.
  • Purchases: subscription/entitlement status is handled by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat when you purchase Plus.
  • No AdMob / no ad SDKs in this app.
  • No app account and no server collection of statements or subscription lists in the MVP.

Statement imports

PDF/email import in the MVP is a stub that stores mock parse results locally. It is not live bank or mailbox parsing. Future real parsing should keep statement contents on-device unless you explicitly opt into a cloud feature (none in this MVP).

Third parties

  • Google Play - distribution and billing
  • RevenueCat - purchase entitlement management

See their respective privacy policies for purchase-related processing.

Children

Sub Radar is not directed at children under 13.
