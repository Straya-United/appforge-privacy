DayFive Privacy Policy

AppForge Digital · Package com.appforge.dayfive · Last updated: 10 September 2026

DayFive helps you triage exactly five next actions for today. This policy describes what the Android MVP collects and how it is used.

Data we process on your device

  • Sticky notes, paste dumps, and triage state (Done / Snooze / Kill, daily action counts) are stored locally with Capacitor Preferences. They are used to show today’s five cards.
  • Calendar and mail stubs in this MVP are simulated sample items. DayFive does not connect to Gmail or Outlook yet and does not collect OAuth tokens in this build.

Purchases

Optional Plus subscriptions/lifetime unlocks use Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. Purchase history and entitlement status for the plus entitlement may be processed by Google and RevenueCat to unlock features. We do not use AdMob or serve third-party ads.

Permissions

Internet access is used for Play Billing / RevenueCat when you purchase or restore. The MVP does not request microphone or contacts access.

Children

DayFive is not directed at children under 13.
