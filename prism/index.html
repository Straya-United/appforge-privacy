Privacy Policy — Prism

Last updated: 29 September 2026
App: com.appforgedigital.prism (Android and iOS)
Contact: admin@appforgedigital.com.au

Overview. Prism is a colour logic puzzle game: paint a grid red, yellow and blue using the mixed-colour diamonds as clues. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no online play and no servers of its own. Your progress is stored on your device and is never uploaded. The game is free to play and supported by ads, with an optional one-time purchase that removes them.

What the game stores

Which puzzles you have solved and your best times, the puzzles you are part-way through (including your notes), your Daily puzzle results and streak, how many hints you have used, and your settings (sound, vibration, mistake highlighting, colour symbols and timer). All of it is kept in the app's own local storage on your device. Prism does not upload it, and we cannot see it.

There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes everything the game has stored.

Advertising

Prism shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. A banner appears on the menu screens, and an occasional full-screen ad may appear after you solve a puzzle — never while you are solving one. To serve and measure those ads, Google receives information from your device, which may include your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system details, coarse location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with an ad. This information is handled by Google, not by us; see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised advertising, in your device settings under Settings › Privacy › Ads. On iOS the game asks for permission to track before it first prepares an ad (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared and ads are still shown, just not personalised. You can change this later under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Purchases

Removing ads is a one-time purchase made through Google Play Billing on Android or the Apple App Store on iOS. The purchase is processed by Google or Apple, and its status is validated through our billing provider, RevenueCat, so the app knows the purchase is active. This involves purchase and receipt records and an anonymous identifier RevenueCat assigns to the installation.

We never see or receive your payment details. The app does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal detail.

Puzzles, hints and the Daily puzzle

Every puzzle ships inside the app. Hints are worked out on your device, and the Daily puzzle is chosen from the date on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere, there is no leaderboard and no server checks your answers — it all works the same offline.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load ads and check purchase status.
  • Advertising ID (Android) / Tracking permission (iOS) — used by the AdMob SDK to deliver and measure ads, as described above.
  • Billing — to make the Remove Ads purchase through Google Play or the App Store.
  • Vibration — for the light tap when you paint a square (can be turned off in Settings).

The game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions.

Playing offline

All 440 puzzles, the Daily puzzle, notes and hints work without a network connection. Ads and purchases need one; if the device is offline, Prism simply carries on without them, and a Remove Ads purchase you have already made stays active.

Children

Prism is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

Settings › Reset progress clears your solved puzzles, streak and stats. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes everything the game has stored on your device. A Remove Ads purchase is tied to your Google account or Apple ID and can be restored from the game's Settings after reinstalling.

Changes
